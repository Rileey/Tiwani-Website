What it does

Three jobs, done properly.

Member directory

Profiles, roles and contact details in one searchable register — filter by active, pending, owing or overdue and see a member's whole standing at a glance.

Events & RSVPs

Create a meeting or picnic, invite the roll, and watch attendance come in. Month and week views keep the calendar honest — effortlessly.

Dues, voting & updates

Charge dues, levies, fines and pledges; run contribution pools; put motions to a poll; and broadcast the outcome to everyone at once.

A closer look

The tabs your officers will live in.

01 — Home

The state of the association, on open.

Four figures answer the questions a secretary is always asked: how many of us are there, what is coming up, how much has been collected, and who is behind. Quick actions sit underneath so the common jobs are one tap deep.

  • Members · Events · Collected · Overdue at a glance
  • Quick actions: add member, new event, new poll, record pay
  • Library and document upload for the constitution and minutes
02 — Members

A register that keeps itself tidy.

Search by name, email or phone. Filter the roll by membership state or by money owed. Every member carries their role, their status and their balance, so nobody has to cross-reference a spreadsheet before a meeting.

  • Filters: all, active, pending, inactive, suspended, paid, owing, overdue
  • Roles and permissions — admins, executives, ordinary members
  • Invite by link; members complete their own profile
03 — Finance

Dues and pools, kept to the penny.

Two ledgers: dues for what the association charges, contributions for what it pools. Charge dues, levies, donations, fines, pledges or anything else; record payments as they arrive; tap any figure to break it down by member or charge type.

  • Outstanding, charged and collected — always reconciled
  • Contribution pools with expected amounts and withdraw requests
  • My Ledger: every member sees their own statement
04 — Vote

Decisions on the record, not in the chat.

Put a motion, an election or a book club pick to the membership. Options can carry images, polls can close on a date, and the tally is visible to everyone — so the outcome is a record rather than an argument.

  • Elections with races and a secret ballot, alongside ordinary polls
  • Open and closed polls with expiry dates
  • Live percentages per option
  • One member, one vote — tied to the register
05 — Events & Market

From the calendar to the door.

An event carries its own page: published status, date, place, a note, the capacity and who is going. Members RSVP from that page — 0 going, 30 spots left becomes 1 going, 29 spots left — and on the day an admin checks attendees in by name. Market is the members' own notice board.

  • Week strip, month view and an upcoming list
  • RSVP, capacity and an attendee list drawn from the register
  • Admin check-in at the door; edit or cancel with everyone notified
  • Members see the same page with RSVP only — no edit, no cancel
  • Member-to-member listings inside the association
06 — Library & governance

The constitution, the minutes, the paper trail.

Upload the by-laws, the code of conduct, the finance reports and every set of minutes. Documents are filed under constitutional papers or minutes and reports, tagged, dated and searchable — published to members, or held back while a revision is in progress.

  • Two shelves: constitutional documents, minutes & reports
  • Published or draft, with file type, size and date on the card
  • Admin tools alongside: join requests, audit logs, deletion requests
07 — Market

A marketplace that stays inside the association.

Members list what they are selling — photograph, condition, description, price — and the rest of the register browses it. Tiwani does not take the money or hold the goods; Enquire simply opens a text message or WhatsApp to the seller, so the deal is arranged between two members who already know each other.

  • Listings by members and admins, with availability status
  • Enquire by text message or WhatsApp — no in-app payments
  • Marketplace rules published under Settings

How to use Tiwani

Nine steps from an empty chapter to a running association.

Scroll — the screen follows the step.

Step one

Sign in and name the association

Get Started, then sign in — or send Request to Join if the register already exists and an admin should let you in. Set the currency, the timezone and who holds admin, and the home dashboard is ready to fill.

Step two

Add or invite your members

Tap the person-plus icon on Members to add someone by hand, or send an invite link and let them complete their own profile. Assign roles as you go — admins can charge and record, members can view and vote.

Step three

Set dues and open a pool

On Finance, choose New Dues and pick the charge type — dues, levies, donations, fines, pledges. For a project, open a contribution pool with an expected amount per member. Both ledgers update the moment money lands.

Step four

Schedule an event and invite the roll

Press plus on Events, set the date and any attendance fee, then invite the whole register or a subset. RSVPs come back against member names, and the fee appears on each invitee's ledger.

Step five

Publish it and collect RSVPs

Once published, the event page shows the date, the place, the note and the capacity — 0 going, 30 spots left. Members tap RSVP to This Event and their name joins the attendee list; you can edit or cancel from the same page.

Step six

Check members in at the door

On the day, open Event Check-In. Every RSVP is listed with a single Check In button, and the header keeps the count honest — one RSVP, none checked in, until people start arriving.

Step seven

Put the motion to a vote

New Poll from the home screen: a question, the options, and a closing date. Members vote once each; percentages update live; the closed poll stays on file as the minute of the decision.

Step eight

Record payments, single or bulk

Record Payment takes one member or a whole batch. Filter to paid, unpaid or overdue, tick the members who have settled, and every outstanding figure on the dashboard moves with them.

Step nine

File the minutes in the Library

Upload the minutes and the finance report, tag them, publish them. Members read the papers from their phones instead of asking for them — and the constitution stops living in one officer's inbox.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do members need to pay online through the app?

No. Tiwani is a register, not a payment processor by default — cash, bank transfer and card payments are all recorded against the member who made them, so the ledger reflects however your association actually collects.

Can more than one person administer the register?

Yes. Roles are set per member, so a chairman, secretary and treasurer can each hold admin rights while ordinary members keep view-and-vote access to their own ledger and the polls.

What happens to members who never open the app?

They stay on the register. An admin can add them by hand, record their payments, and mark their RSVPs — the figures stay correct whether or not every member installs anything.

Which currency and timezone does it use?

Both are set per association under Settings, so a union with members in Lagos and London keeps one currency on the books and each member sees events in their own time.

Is there a web version for the executives?

The register is shared, so anything recorded on a phone is immediately visible to every other officer. Talk to us about desktop access for reporting and end-of-year returns.

How do we move off our spreadsheet?

Bring the sheet to the demo. We will map your columns to member fields, charge types and outstanding balances, and load the opening figures so your first month in Tiwani reconciles with your last month out of it.
